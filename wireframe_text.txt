Increase unique DecodeXtreme registrations. Keep IEEEXtreme education and IEEE membership promotion as supporting
pathways.

How to read this pack
Boxes describe content and placement. Dates and audience rules are confirmed. People, partners, closing deadline, video,
music, and final challenge policies are pending.

Prepared for sharing. Screen layouts are schematic and use neutral wireframe typography; final branding and motion are
developed next.

14 / Privacy, delivery & launch
The remaining scope and a practical route from approved wireframes to a working website.

PRIVACY & PARTICIPANT COMMUNICATION PAGE
State collected data, event-management purpose, who can access it, retention arrangements and query contact. Explain
WhatsApp number visibility. Link from forms and footer; avoid collecting unnecessary data.

DELIVERY SEQUENCE

1 / Review these wireframes
Confirm hierarchy, routes, forms and the revised video hero.

2 / Inspect the React template
Check structure, dependencies and fit; reuse useful parts.

3 / Build the visual system
Logos, proposed fonts, glass surfaces, light/dark themes, video and motion.

4 / Build pages & registration
Private Google Sheets integration, validation, retries and email delivery.

5 / Verify & publish
Mobile, accessibility, performance, approved content, live registration check.

ASSETS & DECISIONS STILL NEEDED
React template; background video and poster; optional music track; common closing date/time; speakers and proctor details;
committee photos/names; past ambassador years; approved contacts, sponsors, prizes/certificates; owner Gmail, email sender
and hosting details.

Design may proceed with honest placeholders. Final rules, working registration, approved content and required assets must be
ready before public launch.
